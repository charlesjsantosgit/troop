# Roots & Rockets: shared societies

Choose **PLAY ONLINE · SHARED TOWNS**. Everyone connects to the same economy.
The first time you enter, a small guide shows your next step. Press **B →
Tutorial** to pause, resume or choose another lesson. You can play freely at any
time. **WASD** moves, the mouse looks around, and **E** talks or works nearby.

## Your first day

1. Follow the marker to **Nana**, the Earth market keeper, and press E.
2. Visit her market desk and buy a banana. Prices and your available goods are
   visible before you buy or sell.
3. Visit the **community board** and accept **A welcome for the neighborhood**.
4. Bring eight bananas to the market and give them for the request. Your actual
   goods leave your bag and the funded reward enters your wallet.

The journal holds directions and information. A person's menu shows that
person's work, requests and services. A bed's menu handles that bed. Walk to a
workplace before using its controls; a waypoint is a direction, not a teleport.

Use **B → Places** for town boards and local neighbors. Roads connect the Earth
towns. Each society has its own shops, municipal stocks, workers and equipment.
Your wallet follows you across all towns. You have one Earth bag and one Moon
bag, shared across the towns on that world.

New authenticated residents receive 1,800 credits once from the municipal
fund. A town claim costs 750 credits, and each player may claim one town.
Visit an unclaimed board and choose **Claim this town**. Its owner can manage
their beds, hire workers and maintain utilities. Visitors can still shop and
complete their own requests. Owning an Earth town does not grant its paired
Moon town. Claims are exclusive and survive reconnects and server restarts.

## Growing food

Choose **Grow food** in the tutorial. At an empty bed you own, select a crop for
which you have the correct seed or start. Planting consumes it. Watering uses
five water; fertilizer uses nutrients. The bed tells you what is missing.
Sprouts grow into leaves, flowers and ripe produce, with different shapes for
leaf crops, roots, grains and fruit. Harvest when the crop reaches maturity,
then bring the produce to a market or use it in a request or recipe.

Plant detail is highest nearby and becomes simpler at distance. Town trees are
placed clear of service roads and work areas. Automatic growers still need
real planting stock, water, nutrients, reachable work and wages.

## Practical jobs and deliveries

Meet workers in person to see what they are doing and what they need. Owners
can hire them for practical occupations at that worker's menu. If a worker is
already carrying goods, their current delivery finishes before a new job takes
over. Work motions follow the real task; waiting does not simulate production.

The rig consumes machinery fuel and extracts finite crude. Refinery crews use
crude and water to make distinct gasoline, diesel, jet fuel and byproducts.
Drivers carry real goods to the gas station, aircraft supply and marine depot.
The delivery jeeps have actual wheels, tire forces, acceleration, steering,
braking and loading bays. They yield to obstacles and pedestrians. Cargo stays
aboard until the vehicle reaches its destination and stops; a blocked road does
not award an invisible delivery. Keep loading bays clear.

The **Make & deliver** lesson introduces the workshop and a delivery driver.
Recipes show their ingredients and timed outputs. Player vehicles also use fuel;
park at a matching depot and use its menu to buy the correct fuel.

## Moon life and cargo

Use the **Moon life** tutorial to find the rocket hatch and travel normally.
Moon services cluster around paths: the greenhouse entrance, beds, market,
habitat, cargo terminal, solar panels and nearby ice operation. Each physical
service point uses the same coordinates for menus and server checks.

Lunar crops require pressure, water and electricity. Panels generate in
sunlight; batteries support the greenhouse in darkness. A town owner can use
solar kits, expand battery capacity and repair equipment. The array and
greenhouse menus show the current supply and needs.

Travel does not copy your Earth bag into your Moon bag. At a cargo terminal,
ship owned goods using packaging and a paid carrier service. Goods occupy the
shipment until arrival and consume destination capacity. Freight fees, stock,
requests and wages all transfer finite resources.

## Space and saves

The same credited photographic sky appears on the Moon and in vacuum during
both rocket voyages. Terrain relief blocks the Sun across crater walls even
beyond nearby dynamic shadow maps. Planet targets are approximate visual
references; this is not a live astronomical ephemeris. Ground and illuminated
surfaces use normal exposure; observation mode makes faint sky objects easier
to study.

Online ownership, accounts, cargo, crops and work live on the server's
persistent storage with an atomic checkpoint and previous backup. Local
**Offline career** saves stay separate. Tutorial progress is local to your
installation and can be replayed without resetting your game.

Install or apply **0.6.1** and restart before joining. Every client and the
server must use **0.6.1 / protocol 13**. Restart after updating; an older running copy cannot connect.
Players with installations older than 0.4.10 need the full installer.

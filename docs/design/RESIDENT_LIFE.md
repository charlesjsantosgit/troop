# Resident life: food, home, clinic and legal careers

Resident life connects the existing backpack, owned homes, clinics and paid city jobs. Nutrition, hydration and rest begin at 80/100 and decline only while that resident is actively connected and being advanced by the authority. Their floor is 20. The only proposed movement effect is a multiplier from 0.85 to 1.0, recovered by tending to needs; needs never cause damage or starvation death.

Food consumes exactly one real Earth backpack item through `FrontierSim._consume`, including the resource ledger. Water restores 40 hydration, bananas restore 18 nutrition and 4 hydration, and prepared meals restore 40 nutrition and 10 hydration. The fixed consumables catalog also accepts a small selection of existing produce and dried food. Eating at full relevant needs fails without spending an item.

An owned residential bed starts 15 seconds of rest. Active updates restore four rest points per second while the resident stays within three metres of the server-known bed. Moving away or reaching the end cancels recovery. There is no offline recovery or punishment. This first implementation is a short bedside rest interaction; animation or furniture occupancy can be layered over it without changing the durable need model.

A clinic within 18 metres charges 18 existing credits and transfers them to the municipal treasury. Treatment raises deficient needs to 70 and returns a server-authorized `heal_to: 100` effect for the controller/network health hook. It has a 30-second repeat guard. Players can instead recover through food, water and home rest. When actual health is known, a full-health, adequately supplied resident does not get charged for needless treatment. The existing multiplayer combat health lives on the client Player; this module authorizes paid healing but does not move the whole combat health model to the server. When the server does not know current health, it allows an otherwise valid paid treatment.

Courier, maintenance and provisioning careers track successful completed work, with provisioning including depot restocks. Levels unlock at 0, 3, 8, 16 and 30 completions, offering 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 percent extra pay on subsequent jobs of that career. Bonuses are whole credits rounded down and transfer from treasury only if sufficient funds remain after existing job commitments. An unfunded bonus is skipped but progress is still earned. Base job rewards keep their existing funded-cargo rules. Completion serials prevent repeated progress or bonuses after a retry or load.

## Authority integration contract

- Store the actor-keyed dictionary at `city.state.resident_life`; `ResidentLife.new_state()` returns an empty dictionary. `migrate` accepts a missing legacy field; `valid` rejects malformed existing records. Maximum 512 records, each with fixed fields and three bounded career counters. No per-census-population records are created.
- Create a `ResidentLife` instance and assign its `state` to that dictionary. Persist its resulting state through the ordinary city transaction and save paths. `view(actor)` returns only that actor's needs and career information and does not allocate a record.
- Call `advance(actor, dt, position, authority_time, context)` only for active residents. Each call caps elapsed time at five seconds and has no catch-up loop. Never call it for disconnected players or for the aggregate census. Context must describe the actor's current authoritative physical room and bed.
- Dispatch `action(kind, payload, sim, actor, position, authority_time, context)` for `life_consume {item}`, `life_rest {building}`, and `life_clinic {building}`. Never copy physical context from client payloads. All failed life actions leave model, money and inventory state unchanged.
- Bed context: `{bed: {building, position: Vector3, owner: actor, residential: true}}`. Clinic context: `{clinic: {building, position: Vector3, kind: "clinic"}, health: actual_health}`. The caller resolves building and furniture from server-known city/room state, including emergency closure and room access checks. The model independently validates identity, type, ownership, physical range and authority time.
- Apply a successful, authority-approved clinic `heal_to` result to the player through the existing health path. Server validation covers location, payment and repeat guard; existing client-owned combat health is not made authoritative by this hook. Apply `view(actor).stamina_multiplier` to the intended mild movement/recovery effect. The pure model itself does not access scene nodes.
- Call `record_job(actor, active.id, active.kind, active.reward, sim, time, reserved_payroll)` only after successful delivery, base payment and removal of the completed active job. `reserved_payroll` is the sum of other active job rewards. There is no public action for granting career progress. Save its result atomically with the ordinary job completion.
- Merge `view(actor)` into `city_view.resident_life`. `ResidentLifePanel.build(parent, controller, view, context)` adds a compact live needs/food/career card and contextual rest/clinic buttons. The helper calls `controller.request_action`; it does not mutate inventory or local needs. It refreshes twice per second only while visible.

## Validation

Run the registered main-scene mode with `godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . -- residentlifetest`. It verifies migration and bad saves, active-time bounds, real inventory consumption and resource conservation, failed-action atomicity, remote/forged service rejection, timed rest and cancellation, clinic funding, career thresholds, payroll reservation, replay protection and concrete UI dispatch. Broader city integration and two-client scene replication require their own integration gates; this module test does not imply those have passed.

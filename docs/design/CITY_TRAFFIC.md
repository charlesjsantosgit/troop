# Crownreach street traffic

Implemented September 5, 2026. This is a coherent fictional U.S. urban driving profile informed by FHWA traffic-control conventions and New York guidance. It is not a claim that a generated city has satisfied every federal, state, or local law, or the engineering studies required for a real road installation.

## Primary research

- [FHWA current MUTCD edition](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/kno_11th_Editionr1.htm): the current manual is the 11th Edition with Revision 1, December 2025. The [official complete PDF](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Editionr1/mutcd11theditionr1hl.pdf) was downloaded and its relevant sections read directly. Section 4F.17 specifies yellow after green and discusses engineered yellow/red-clearance timing. Section 4I.06 describes WALK, flashing-hand clearance, a steady-hand buffer, and pedestrian timing. Crownreach uses seven seconds of WALK, a slower 1 m/s crossing design speed, and a three-second steady-hand buffer. Sections 2B and 3B informed the signs, stop bars, and white crosswalk markings.
- [New York DMV: Traffic Control](https://dmv.ny.gov/new-york-state-drivers-manual-and-practice-tests/chapter-4-traffic-control): red/amber/green display order, stopping before a stop line or crosswalk, and the NYC restriction on turns at red signals informed the actor rules. Both right and left turns on red are prohibited here.
- [New York DMV: Intersections and Turns](https://dmv.ny.gov/new-york-state-drivers-manual-and-practice-tests/chapter-5-intersections-and-turns): vehicles yield to occupied intersections and pedestrians; left turns yield to opposing traffic; simultaneous stop arrivals yield to the right; entry waits when the receiving lane lacks room.
- [NYC311: Speed Limits](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-01368) describes 25 mph unless otherwise posted. Crownreach explicitly posts 25 mph on avenues and a fictional, lower 15 mph neighborhood limit on local streets. The [NYC DOT slow-zone program](https://home4.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/motorist/slowzones.shtml) is context for varying posted neighborhood limits, rather than a claim that every real NYC local street has a 15 mph limit.

## Implemented behavior

`city_traffic.gd` owns the road graph and intersection state. `city_crowd.gd` provides the actual CharacterBody vehicles and pedestrians. `city_traffic_signals.gd` reads the same state to power instanced lenses, pedestrian symbols, and clearance countdown digits. There is no separate decorative signal clock.

The graph follows CityPlan's rectangular `STREET_SPACING` (104.4672 by 298.32 m). Streets are 24 m wide; there are no roads through the sixteen building parcels inside each block. Traffic stays in the right-hand lane 2.7 m from the centerline. Sparse local streets have alternating one-way directions, enforced by graph edges and shown by ONE WAY and DO NOT ENTER signs. Internal park roads, the plaza, unsupported edges, wrong-way entries, and U-turns are excluded from route selection. Routes choose successive legal edges rather than repeating a fixed square.

Avenue junctions use north/south green, three-second yellow, two-second all-red, east/west green, yellow, all-red, an exclusive pedestrian WALK interval, crossing clearance, and the steady-hand buffer. All red clearance intervals remain red if an admitted vehicle or pedestrian has not finished. New pedestrians start only on WALK; a pedestrian already crossing continues during the flashing hand. Countdown digits are shown only during clearance. On the widest avenue crossing, clearance is 27 seconds for a 26.4 m curb-to-curb route.

Local junctions use STOP and ALL WAY signs. Cars make a full stationary stop, retain arrival order, and yield to simultaneous arrivals on their right. In the special case of four simultaneous arrivals, three drivers defer to one car after a three-second standstill, avoiding indefinite cyclic priority. This courteous tie resolution does not release multiple conflicting cars.

Drivers accelerate at 1.8 m/s² and normally brake at 3.2 m/s². They reduce speed for turns, respect posted limits, preserve a 2.2 m stopped bumper gap and a moving headway, wait for an occupied intersection, yield before a permissive left turn, and avoid entering a blocked receiving lane. Yellow admits a moving vehicle too close to stop comfortably; a stationary vehicle never starts on yellow. Physical sweeps and collisions stop vehicles for people and obstacles even when those obstacles are not traffic-model actors. Cars have working brake lights, directional turn indicators, headlamps, wheel animation, glazing, and drivers.

District services are copied to the controller through `Crowd.set_district_services(rows)` at the city UI refresh frequency, not queried by every vehicle. Zero electrical supply changes heads to flashing red and blanks pedestrian displays; all-way-stop and pedestrian-yield rules take over. Restoration first shows a complete all-red/steady-hand buffer. Mobility deterioration lowers the desired speed without raising the posted limit or bypassing a signal.

## Driving integration review

`CityCar` uses the existing rigid-body engine, raycast suspension, load-sensitive tire forces, drivetrain, brakes, reverse interlocks and parked support. Each catalog class sets its own metre-scale body, wheelbase, wheel radius, mass, torque curve and braking torque. Suspension spring and damping rates scale with mass; steering, anti-roll coupling and driven axles are configured for the road-car class. The renderer rotates its -Z model forward axis against the solver's +Z convention. The driver's root, recline, four control targets, seat camera and exits derive from the actual canonical full-size cabin fit. Traffic actors retain their bounded route-following acceleration/braking model; player-owned cars use the independent rigid-body drivetrain. The root integration gate separately checks real acceleration, slalom, braking and cabin fit across all ten models.

## Vehicle impacts

`Vehicle._integrate_forces` reads actual contact positions, normals and impulses; normal closing speed gates `report_collision_impact`. The continuous city aircraft sweep uses the same callback for unloaded facades. Hard braking without a contact no longer creates an impact. [Godot's direct body state reference](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_physicsdirectbodystate3d.html) defines these contact and impulse APIs.

Impacts deform independent vehicle mesh copies locally, preserve the original shared fleet assets and occupants, emit bounded fragments, smoke and a brief light flash, and reduce available propulsion. Severe impacts disable boarding and propulsion; a wrecked jet cannot restore turbine thrust or afterburner. The rigid-body solver handles the chassis, while visual fragments sweep real collision geometry. Aircraft swept hits remove incoming normal speed and retain dissipative tangential motion. Building incidents receive the same measured impact through the city's authoritative adapter.

`vehicleimpacttest` passes 27/27 headless collision, deformation and aircraft-breakup assertions; its native capture passes 30/30. Severe aircraft impacts add six detached groups copied from the actual aircraft mesh, a burned central remnant, a brief fireball and dark smoke, bounded to three breakup events and twelve seconds. At most eight effect bursts contain 192 fragments, 160 smoke puffs and sixteen mesh draws; all expire within eight seconds. Vehicle damage and wreck condition now follow the existing registered driver’s authorized movement and reliable seat-release messages. The server accepts only the claimed canonical vehicle, validates a contact payload capped at 512 bytes, prevents damage rollback, and broadcasts monotonic reliable revisions. A fatal ejection includes crash state before the next 20 Hz update. Replica updates change vehicle presentation and propulsion without inflicting duplicate pilot or building damage. Late joins receive pages of at most 32 records / 16 KB and build the burned aircraft remnant without replaying old explosions. This follows the existing driver-authoritative vehicle physics; it does not independently resimulate every client collision on the server. Wreck state lasts for the current server session; building-incident persistence is separate. This is a rigid-body impact model with localized visual deformation, not a finite-element structural or fuel-combustion simulation. The comprehensive traffic gate retains one existing Dummy renderer shader RID cleanup warning; the focused traffic-impact gate exits cleanly.

Recovery restores the shared shell and resumes legal acceleration at the same position.
